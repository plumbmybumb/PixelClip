# Credits and third-party material

PixelClip's own code, documentation, screenshots, content and case design files are released under the
[MIT licence](../LICENSE). For the documentation and screenshots this is the licence's "associated documentation
files"; for the case files see also [`../case/README.md`](../case/README.md#licence-and-no-warranty).

GitHub labels the repository "MIT", but a large share of its bytes is material that keeps its own terms. This page
lists everything in the repository that the project did not author, and the project's own material that a reader
might mistake for third-party work.

## Material from others, not bundled

- **ESP-IDF** and the registry components `esp_lcd_sh8601`, `joltwallet/littlefs` and `espressif/mdns` (with
  `espressif/cmake_utilities`) are downloaded by the firmware build, each under its own licence. Exact versions are
  locked in [`../firmware/dependencies.lock`](../firmware/dependencies.lock).
- **Waveshare's** schematic, board STEP model and demo code are referred to in [`HARDWARE.md`](HARDWARE.md) and
  [`MECHANICAL.md`](MECHANICAL.md) but are not included. `docs/mechanical/board-top.svg` is the project's own
  drawing of the dimensions extracted from the model.

## Small pieces with an outside origin

- **Display initialisation table** (`firmware/components/display/display.c`): the seven RM67162 panel commands are
  taken from Waveshare's demo for the ESP32-S3-AMOLED-1.91 (`example_qspi_with_ram.c`), byte for byte, except that
  the demo's final full-brightness command is dropped. They are standard display-controller commands.
- **5 × 7 bitmap font** (`firmware/components/ui/font5x7.c`, and the same table in `tools/common/dotfont5x7.h`):
  the classic 5 × 7 character table that circulates widely in open-source display code, in the style of
  character-LCD fonts. The project does not know which copy it was taken from and knows of no claim on it.

## The project's own material

- **The 20 starter animation loops** (`ios/Sources/Resources/Starter/*.hca`, listed in `starter.json`) and the test
  animation `swift-orbit.hca` are original to this project, generated procedurally by the project's own scripts.
  They are released under the MIT licence with the rest of the project. The generator scripts are not included in
  this repository.
- **The tarot reading texts** (`ios/Sources/Resources/Tarot/readings.json` and the `reader-*.txt` bundles) are
  written for this project. The way the three cards are read follows the structural method described in
  *The Way of Tarot* by Alejandro Jodorowsky and Marianne Costa.
- **The app icon** is generated by `ios/scripts/make_icon.swift`.
- **The case** (STL, STEP, the Fusion script in `case/source/` and the renders) is the project's own design; see
  [`../case/README.md`](../case/README.md#licence-and-no-warranty) for its licence and the no-warranty note.
